Frequently Asked Questions

Does the app need an internet connection?

An internet connection is required to load map tiles via Apple Maps. All battle content, text, and historical photos are bundled with the app and work offline.

Why does the app ask for no permissions?

Pacific Crucible doesn't use your location, camera, photos, or contacts. It only displays historical maps and content, so no special permissions are required.

I found a historical inaccuracy — how do I report it?

Please email a description of the issue (and the battle/step involved) to the address above. Corrections are always welcome.
