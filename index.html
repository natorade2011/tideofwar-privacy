Privacy Policy for Tide of War

Last updated: July 25, 2026

Tide of War ("the App") is a historical reference app that lets you step through key battles of the Pacific War with maps, narratives, and public-domain photographs.

Data Collection

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Specifically, the App:

  • Does not require an account, login, or sign-up
  • Does not access your location, contacts, camera, or photo library
  • Does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs
  • Does not make network requests to any server operated by us

Third-Party Services

The App uses Apple's MapKit framework to display maps. Apple may collect data as described in Apple's own privacy policy when rendering map tiles; this is governed by Apple, not by us, and is the same behavior as the built-in Maps app.

Content

All historical photographs included in the App are U.S. government works or otherwise in the public domain, sourced from Wikimedia Commons.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13, because it does not collect information from anyone at all.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed via the GitHub repository.
